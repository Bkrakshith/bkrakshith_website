How a programme starts

Five steps. The hardest task is rarely the right first task.

  1. DiscoverPick one task with a measurable end state.
  2. ArchitectSet requirements, safety, and what to buy or build.
  3. SimulateTest layouts and controllers in a digital twin.
  4. PilotRun one instrumented pilot next to normal work.
  5. ScalePlan support, training and the next use case.
