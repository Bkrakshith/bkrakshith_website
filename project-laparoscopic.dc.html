Laparoscopic surgery trainer.

I was the project manager for an eight-month project with a team of eight people and an NHS Trust. The team designed and programmed a compliant robotic mechanism for a laparoscopic surgery trainer. The mechanism gives realistic force feedback to surgeons during practice.

Medical robotics Force feedback Product design Project management
Film
What

This is a new type of laparoscopic surgery trainer. We developed it in eight months with an NHS Trust. A compliant robotic mechanism gives realistic force feedback at the tool. The modules connect to simulate more than one incision. Off-the-shelf parts keep the cost low. A VR system gives trainees the stress of a real operating theatre.

How

I was the project manager, and I divided the team of eight people into sub-groups with clear responsibilities. The team had regular gateway reviews with the NHS Trust. The control system was the most difficult part, because the mechanism must stay compliant and also control the tool accurately. A PID controller and servo angle control closed the loop. The team added a soft tool point where the servo torque was above the displacement threshold. My individual technical work was the CAD model.

Results

At the end of the eight months, the team delivered a working demonstrator. It is modular, low in cost and has VR integration. It helps with two problems in laparoscopic training: there are not sufficient trained instructors, and there is no standard qualification route.
