Robotics solutions architect · Generalist

Hands-on with real robots, across hardware and software.

Rakshith Bangalore was involved in starting a robotics programme at Nottingham Trent University. The work is hands-on: running demos, teleoperating the robots, building reinforcement learning pipelines, and reading and testing different methods to make humanoid robots work in industrial settings. Latest project Open source · In development · Simulation only
WardZero

The simulated hospital where robots train before they meet patients.

WardZero is an open platform, created by Rakshith, to develop and test robot solutions for hospitals. It provides a full simulated hospital with a surgical wing, two simulators, a safety layer and navigation. The reference robot is a Unitree G1 humanoid with two BrainCo Revo2 hands.

Profile

Rakshith is a robotics solutions architect and hands-on generalist. The experience covers humanoid robot R&D for PepsiCo, mobile manipulation for Walkers, medical robotics with an NHS Trust, robot simulation, and hardware and software product delivery.

Approach

A system rarely fails because of the model. It fails because of the parts around the model. Rakshith identifies the system before trusting it, on a humanoid actuator and in an AI product alike.
