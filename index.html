Rakshith Bangalore — Rakshith

Robotics engineer · Entrepreneur

Robots, and the intelligence that makes them work.

Rakshith Bangalore is a robotics engineer and entrepreneur in the United Kingdom and Singapore. The work covers humanoid robots, robot simulation, mobile manipulators and AI products. One principle connects all of it: a model must work in the real system, not only in simulation.

Latest project Open source · In development · Simulation only
WardZero

The simulated hospital where robots train before they meet patients.

WardZero is an open platform, created by Rakshith, to develop and test robot solutions for hospitals. It provides a full simulated hospital with a surgical wing, two simulators, a safety layer and navigation. The reference robot is a Unitree G1 humanoid with two BrainCo Revo2 hands.

Profile

Rakshith is a robotics engineer with experience in humanoid robot R&D, mobile manipulation and manufacturing automation. Rakshith now manages the delivery of an AI-enabled full-stack software product and a portable podcasting solution.

Approach

A system rarely fails because of the model. It fails because of the parts around the model. Rakshith identifies the system before trusting it, on a humanoid actuator and in an AI product alike.
