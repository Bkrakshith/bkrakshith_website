Profile

A robotics engineer who builds intelligence into machines. Several years across humanoid R&D, mobile manipulation and manufacturing automation, now project manager for PodCut and GoBox. Based between the United Kingdom and Singapore.

Approach

The model is rarely what makes or breaks a system. Everything around it is. A controller that holds up in simulation and fails against a real actuator is the same failure as an AI feature that demos well and falls over on the fifth job of the day. I identify the system before I trust it.

Selected work

Robotics, end to end.

From mechanical design and integration through to control, learning and validation on real hardware.

01

Industry 4.0 mobile manipulator

MEng capstone · Scoped with Walkers

A Universal Robots UR-10 arm on an Omron LD250 autonomous base, in a custom frame enclosing the power systems and controller, with a SmartShift tool changer for swapping end effectors. Scoped with a senior automation director around the real workflow of a Leicester crisp factory — reloading seasoning, palletising cases, and leak-detection over the fryers. Designed in Fusion 360 and physically integrated; pick-and-place validated on a conveyor rig.

02

Humanoid robotics

PepsiCo · Unitree H1-2 & G1

Use-case programming on humanoid platforms: training neural policies for legged control and working the sim-to-real gap on real hardware, where a controller that behaves in simulation comes apart against a real actuator. Actuator identification, and the failure modes that only appear once a controller leaves simulation.

03

Laparoscopic surgery trainer

Project manager · 8-month build

Project manager on an eight-month build of a next-generation trainer: a compliant robotic mechanism designed and programmed to give surgeons realistic force feedback during practice.

04

Autonomous palletising robots

Industrial automation

Designed autonomous mobile robots for industrial palletising, moving packed cases through a working production environment.
