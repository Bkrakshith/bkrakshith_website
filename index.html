The operating theatre in the WardZero surgical wing. Isaac Sim 6.0, path traced.
WardZero

The simulated hospital where robots train before they meet patients.

WardZero is an open platform to develop and test robot solutions for hospitals. It gives a full simulated hospital with a surgical wing, two simulators (Isaac Sim and MuJoCo), a safety layer and navigation. The reference robot is a Unitree G1 humanoid with two BrainCo Revo2 hands.

All parts use the low-level interface of the real robot. Thus, the same software runs in simulation and on the robot.

Profile

I am a robotics engineer. I work on humanoid R&D, mobile manipulation and manufacturing automation. I am now the project manager for PodCut and GoBox. I work between the United Kingdom and Singapore.

Approach

The model is rarely the cause when a system fails. The parts around it are the cause. A controller can work in simulation and fail on a real actuator. An AI feature can work in a demo and fail on the fifth job of the day. Thus, I identify the system before I trust it.
