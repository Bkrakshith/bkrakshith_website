About · Profile and career history

Rakshith Bangalore

Robotics engineer and entrepreneur in the United Kingdom and Singapore. Rakshith puts intelligence into machines, from humanoid robots and mobile manipulators to AI products in production.

Profile

Rakshith Bangalore is a robotics engineer. The work covers humanoid robot R&D, robot simulation, mobile manipulation and manufacturing automation. Rakshith holds an MEng from Nottingham Trent University.

For two years, Rakshith programmed industrial use cases on Unitree H1-2 and G1 humanoid robots for a PepsiCo programme. The work included neural policies for legged control, sim-to-real transfer and actuator identification.

Rakshith now manages the delivery of two products: an AI-enabled full-stack software platform for post-production, and a portable podcasting solution. Rakshith is also the author of WardZero, an open simulation platform for hospital robots, and the host of the Unfiltered Robotics podcast.

“Identify the system before you trust it.”

A controller can work in simulation and fail on a real actuator. An AI feature can work in a demo and fail on the fifth job of the day. In both cases, the parts around the model cause the failure. Rakshith applies the same method to robots and to software.
