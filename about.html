Rakshith Bangalore

Rakshith Bangalore Kumaraswamy · Robotics solutions architect · United Kingdom and Singapore

A robotics solutions architect and hands-on robotics generalist. Rakshith built a robotics programme at Nottingham Trent University from the ground up, and now helps startups and businesses start their own, across robot hardware and software.

Profile

Rakshith Bangalore (full name Rakshith Bangalore Kumaraswamy) is a robotics solutions architect based in the United Kingdom and Singapore. The work covers humanoid robot R&D, robot simulation, mobile manipulation, manufacturing automation and hardware products. Rakshith holds an MEng from Nottingham Trent University and built a robotics programme there from the ground up.

For two years, Rakshith programmed industrial use cases on Unitree H1-2 and G1 humanoid robots for a PepsiCo programme. The work included neural policies for legged control, sim-to-real transfer and actuator identification.

Rakshith also delivers products end to end: an AI-enabled full-stack software platform, and a portable hardware product from prototype to its first production run. Rakshith is the author of WardZero, an open simulation platform for hospital robots, and the host of the Unfiltered Robotics podcast.

Rakshith now works with startups and businesses that want to start a robotics programme: choosing the first use case, architecting the solution, and taking it from pilot to deployment. See the services.

“Identify the system before you trust it.”

A controller can work in simulation and fail on a real actuator. An AI feature can work in a demo and fail on the fifth job of the day. In both cases, the parts around the model cause the failure. Rakshith applies the same method to robots and to software.
