What

I own the delivery of PodCut through its pilot phase. PodCut is an AI post-production SaaS for podcast studios. I manage the sprints and the pilot onboarding. I am also the link between the two engineers and senior management.

How

I use a structured delivery method. I plan and track the sprints, epics and feature sets in JIRA against the project timeline. I record documents and decisions in Confluence. I show the user workflows in Miro, so the team specifies the editing pipeline from the view of the studio before development starts.

Each pilot studio goes through the full onboarding process. First, a discovery call shows if the workflow of the studio is a good fit. Then the studio starts on the platform with a structured onboarding. Regular pilot meetings record problems, and these problems go directly into the sprint backlog.

Results

Two podcast studios use the platform each week. We keep the pilot at two studios while the platform develops. The post-production time for a pilot episode decreased from four days of manual editing to four hours of supervised output. The engineering target is four minutes. At that speed, the video and clips are ready when the recording stops.
