GoBox: a portable studio, from prototype to market.

I manage the product for GoBox, a hardware product that we acquired. I control the production timelines with manufacturers in China. I also manage the pre-sale, the Kickstarter campaign, the lead funnel for the launch and the entry into the US market.

Product management Supply chain BOM · DFM Kickstarter · pre-sale
What

I manage GoBox from start to end. GoBox is a portable recording studio, and we acquired it as a working design. My work starts at production readiness and continues to US market entry. One timeline controls the manufacture, the budget, the campaign and the sales pipeline.

How

Supply: I communicate regularly with the parts manufacturer in China and with the fabricator about the production timelines. The BOM and DFM files are the single source of truth for the product. I also control the budget against the plan.

Demand: I sell GoBox units in a pre-sale before the production run. I manage the Kickstarter campaign. A lead-generation funnel (below) changes interest into pre-orders. For the US market, I prepare the product, the packaging and the fulfilment.

Results

The product is now between the working prototype and its first production run. The manufacture, the campaign and the funnel follow one timeline. The pre-sale is live before the run. The production allocation comes after the pre-sale.
