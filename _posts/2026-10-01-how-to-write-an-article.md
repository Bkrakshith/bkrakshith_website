## Publishing checklist

1. **Title** — the headline. It also becomes the web address (e.g. `/articles/your-title/`), so changing it after sharing will break links. Settle it before publishing.
2. **Summary** — one or two sentences. This is what shows on LinkedIn and in Google, so write it to make people click.
3. **Cover image** — optional, landscape, about 1200×630.
4. **Published** — switch on to put it live. It appears about a minute after saving.

## Formatting

Use the toolbar for **bold**, *italic*, [links](https://unfilteredrobotics.com), lists and headings. Use "Heading 2" for sections and "Heading 3" for sub-sections; the title is already the top heading.

> A quote like this is styled as a pull-quote — good for the one line you want people to remember.

Images go in with the image button and are stored in `assets/articles/`.

To embed a YouTube video, switch the editor to **Source** mode and paste the embed code:

## After publishing

- Post a short teaser on LinkedIn with the link.
- Import it to Medium (Stories → Import a story → paste the link) so Medium credits your site as the original.
