WardZero

WardZero is an open platform to develop and test robot solutions for hospitals. It gives a full simulated hospital with a surgical wing, two simulators, a safety layer and navigation. The reference robot is a Unitree G1 humanoid with two BrainCo Revo2 hands. All parts use the low-level interface of the real robot. Thus, the same software runs in MuJoCo, in Isaac Sim and on the real robot.

Humanoid robotics

I programmed industrial use cases on Unitree H1-2 and G1 humanoid robots. Neural policies control the legs. I trained the policies in simulation and transferred them to the real robots. A controller that works in simulation can fail on a real actuator. Thus, I identified the actuators and recorded the failure modes that occur only on real hardware.

Platforms
Unitree H1-2 · Unitree G1
Stack

02
MEng capstone · Award-winning

Industry 4.0 mobile manipulator

A Universal Robots UR-10 collaborative arm is on an Omron LD250 autonomous mobile base. A custom frame holds the power systems and the robot controller. A SmartShift tool changer replaces the end effectors. I defined the scope with a senior packaging automation director at Walkers, for their crisp factory in Leicester. The robot reloads seasoning, puts packed cases on pallets and moves a leak-detection effector above the oil fryers.

03
University build · NHS Trust collaboration

Laparoscopic surgery trainer

I was the project manager for an eight-month project with a team of eight people. The team designed and programmed a compliant robotic mechanism for a surgery trainer. The mechanism gives realistic force feedback to surgeons during practice. The modules connect to simulate more than one incision. Off-the-shelf parts keep the cost low, and a VR system gives trainees the stress of a real operating theatre.
