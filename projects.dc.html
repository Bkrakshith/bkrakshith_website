WardZero

An open platform to develop and test robot solutions for hospitals. It provides a full simulated hospital with a surgical wing, two simulators, a safety layer and navigation. The same software runs in MuJoCo, in Isaac Sim and on the real robot.

Robot
Unitree G1, 29 DoF · BrainCo Revo2 hands
Simulators
Isaac Sim 6.0 · MuJoCo · MuJoCo MJX
Stack
ROS 2 Humble · Nav2 · slam_toolbox · PPO · ONNX

Humanoid robotics

Rakshith programmed industrial use cases on Unitree H1-2 and G1 humanoid robots. Neural policies control the legs. The work covered sim-to-real transfer, actuator identification and the failure modes that occur only on real hardware.

Platforms
Unitree H1-2 · Unitree G1
Stack
Neural policies for legged control
Focus
Sim-to-real transfer · actuator identification

Industry 4.0 mobile manipulator

A UR-10 collaborative arm on an Omron LD250 autonomous mobile base, with a SmartShift tool changer. Rakshith defined the scope with a senior packaging automation director at Walkers, for the crisp factory in Leicester.

Design

Laparoscopic surgery trainer

Rakshith was the project manager of an eight-person team. The team designed and programmed a compliant robotic mechanism that gives surgeons realistic force feedback. The trainer is modular, low in cost and integrated with VR.

Role
Project manager · team of eight
Mechanism
